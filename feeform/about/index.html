About Feeform

Feeform is a source-backed Alberta GP billing assistant. It suggests supportable billing candidates and missing documentation facts, but it is not claim submission software, legal advice, or the billing authority.

Privacy posture

  • Do not enter names, PHN, DOB, address, phone number, or direct identifiers.
  • The MVP does not store raw encounter text by default.
  • AI provider calls are disabled by default; deterministic extraction is used unless reviewed and configured.
  • Physician or biller final review is required before claim submission.
