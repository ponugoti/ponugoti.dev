Feeform

Turn encounter notes into supportable Alberta billing options.

Do not enter names, PHN, DOB, address, phone number, or other direct identifiers.

Recommendation mode
Billing model?The payment model for the physician. Fee-for-service uses SOMB HSC billing; PCPCM follows a different primary care payment model.
After-hours?Whether the service occurred after hours, on a weekend, or on a holiday. Timing can affect modifier eligibility.
Patient present?Whether the patient was present or directly participating in the service. Some assessment and advice codes depend on this.
Procedure performed?Whether a billable procedure was performed in addition to, or instead of, an assessment.
